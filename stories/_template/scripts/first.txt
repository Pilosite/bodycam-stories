# A first session: what the voice says, and time passing.
#   node tools/story.mjs play <pack> --script <this file>
# `say … => verb target` pins the intent (what the translation tier would answer); without it the heuristic guesses.
# An indented `when … -> …` under a promise is the rule she agrees to keep, in the pack's rule grammar.
lang en
wait 10
say Hello? Can you hear me?
say if you hear a bird, stop for a moment => commit wait
  when sound(bird.call) -> body.stop, say(commit.keeping)
say Go on, walk to the end of the path.
say Look at the bench.
wait 20
