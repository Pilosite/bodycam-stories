## M2 measured: « Louis's Round » with no model

**How to play.** Launch screen: the « Louis's Round » button under « Launch »; or « Plus » → « Story: Louis's Round »; or `?story=louis-round` (`&` options through `window.__CFG`: `story`, `storyOpts { seed, clock: 'manual', cut, lang }`, `lang`). English by default, French with « Plus » → « Language » (kept in the browser); subtitles always. « Plus » → « Story journal » opens the debug panel (state, commitments, engine gaps, last journal entries, « Copy journal »); hidden by default. `window.story` is the test API (`start, hear, advance, look, state, journal, gaps, checkpoint, stop`).

**What works.** The build embeds `core/` (bundled by `tools/bundle-core.mjs`) and the compiled pack. The page implements the executor (`src/adaptateur-core.js`: pure events identical to the paper world, page commands to `src/story.js`). Sam starts at the trailhead and moves only by transitions between adjacent stations: a walk along the path (`trajet` in `src/main.js`, no obstacle logic; a leg that would cross a known trunk or rock becomes a cut) or a cut. The world's clock runs by itself, one `advance(1)` per second (it holds while the player is talking, when the world freezes): the battery drains over 14 minutes, the pack's dwell rules fire (walker at the platière after 70 s, rustle at the hide after 40 s), the light rises on arrival at each station (from the stations' `light`). The player's words go through the input port to the now loop; the model port is served by code (`src/reflex-model.js`: lexical nearest match on `src/reflexes.json` and the pack's `anchors.json`), recorded at tier `reflex`, with no scene loop. Below 0.5 confidence Sam asks back (`ask.back`). Commitments work end to end for the five commit classes (`jay` accepted or amended, `silence`, `battery`, `louis` refused on the path, `leave` always refused). The player's gaze resting on a thing for 1.5 s and 3 s is a `look.at` observation (`session.observe`). Lines come from `lines/en.json` or `fr.json`; the walker speaks with his name and another voice; whisper and `speech.shorten` are applied. The pack's three cues are recipes in `src/bruitages.json` (`indices`). `camera.set` drives a new `uAbime` uniform of `camera.frag` (degradation, camera off) and the shake. `story.end` shows the end screen.

**Measured.** See the M2 row above; the smoke run is `node tools/build.mjs && node tools/story-smoke.mjs` (270 × 480, software rendering).

**What the executor reports as missing** (never faked; reported by `createEngineExecutor().gaps` when the call happens, listed live in the debug panel): `touch` (no hand contact: she looks and bends), `take` (a given thing taken in place: she looks and bends, nothing leaves the ground), `shape` (shaping steps are not in PDL v0), `look` at `dir:*` (no compass in the page), `startScene` (no scene table in the pack), `setPlace` weather, `playSound` for a cue without recipe, `say` of a line missing the play language. Known in `MAPPING` but not reported per call: sounds are not placed in space, tone drives only the whisper, the ground mist of `stations.json` is not applied (no PDL field for it). Data seen in the smoke run: at the hide (heading −90°, posture 0.65) the screen of branches fills the frame and hides the bank; the station's heading or posture in `stations.json` needs a creator's adjustment (about −50°, 0.75).

**Not reached without a model.** The `takeover` and `camera-off` endings are Sam's decisions (a model); with code only, the story ends by the battery. Middle-confidence answers (0.5 to 0.8) act without showing doubt. No middle tier: nothing is invented, every line is from the bank. The prose `!`/`!!` principles stay `to_compile` (not enforced) until a compile step at pack build exists.

## M3, first part: real models behind the page's model port (20 Nov 2026, not yet measured live)

**What is built.** `src/model-port.js` is the page's model port, a chain resolved per call (ADR 0008): the home node when one is configured in this browser and healthy (client side only: `GET /health` with a 1.5 s limit on load, every 30 s and after a failed call; per-capability state; address and token in `localStorage` under `bodycam-home-node`, set by `window.story.homeNode({ url, token })` or by a link `…#home-node=<url>&token=<token>`), else the host's model when the page is a claude.ai artifact (`window.claude.use("sample")`, the same sampler as the demo, `modelTier: 'quick'`), else nothing: the reflex answers alone, as in M2. Judgement (`decide` with typed questions) and invention (`generate`) are cut at 6 s and 20 s and fall to the next adapter, then to a clean failure the core plays as "no answer". The now loop's own questions (intent, reaction) stay with the reflex.

In the core: `core/judge.js` (the confidence rule and both orders, D1 to D3 in one call, D4 on invented lines), `core/prompt.js` (Sam's stage prompt: stable prefix with her principles in scope, examples and counter-examples in the play language, the place, the station, the facts she knows, her promises; small delta; tolerant reading of the token lines a model writes), and `core/clocks.js`: when the reflex is below 0.8 and a model can judge, D1 to D3 are asked once; within 350 ms the now loop uses the answer, else Sam holds (« Hm. Wait. ») and the judged intent lands in the scene loop and amends the reflex one. Middle confidence shows the doubt (« If I got you right… »). When a model can invent, every utterance opens a scene call: her reaction in PDL token form, validated and repaired like any transaction; the first line amends her immediate one; a line a D4 judgement (both orders) finds against a prose absolute, a compiled absolute, or a `story.end` that is not open is rejected and journaled with the principle or the reason. The two endings that are Sam's decision (`takeover`, `camera-off`) are offered to the model once `f.louis-stayed` is established, and reached when it writes `story.end`. A small indicator (top right) says what serves judgement and invention; every `model_call` records `adapter`, `instrument` (judgement or invention), `p`, `play`, latency since the words and `timings.model_ms`; a `summary` entry closes each page session (calls by adapter and instrument, medians, first and full reaction, repairs, rejections); the debug panel shows it live.

**Measured here: nothing live.** There is no model in this container. The unit tests run the whole path with fixed canned answers (`tests/model-port.test.mjs`): they prove the wiring, the fallbacks and the journal, not a latency or a quality.

**How Toine measures it in the artifact.** Publish `dist/artefact.html` as before; open it; « Louis's Round »; check the indicator reads `judge: host · invent: host` (`code · none` means the sampler was not granted). Play ten minutes with free sentences, some the reflex cannot get (« should that blue bird start yelling, flatten yourself »), some about Louis, some rude. « Plus » → « Story journal » → « Copy journal » (after the end screen, so the summary is in it), save it to a file, then `node tools/model-latency.mjs <file>`: p50 and p95 per adapter, instrument and purpose (`model` is the call alone, `since words` is from the end of the sentence), first reaction (the now loop's first act) and full reaction (the scene answer landed), and the summary. In the artifact these are the host's latencies, a showcase (ADR 0004): the tier latencies of M3's exit criterion need the home node. What to look at besides numbers: how often `hold` was said, the `rejected_by` list, `dropped` lines (a model writing something other than PDL), and whether an ending was reached by her decision.

**Known gaps.** No streaming (the host answers whole: `first_ms` = full time). D4 doubles the scene call when an invented line is checked (one more judgement call, both orders in one prompt, which is weaker than two independent calls). The home node server is not built (ADR 0008 open points). No story loop yet (`prepare()` in `core/clocks.js`). The prose `!` principles are given to the model as text, not enforced by code (the compiler is still to come).
