## Deliver
1. **The harvest record** (`core/harvest.js`, pure): from a journal (the core's, and through a small adapter the legacy French journal's entries) build one record: ids and versions (pack, engine build, PDL version), mode (story, free, trailer), device string, play language, duration, turns, the model summary, and the three lists of ADR 0011: **asked and not doable** (executor gaps, refusals for lack of means, intents heard as `other` or below confidence, reserved-v2 rejections), **improvised** (facts, names and things established by a model in play, with the sentence that prompted them), **slow or doubtful** (calls over their loop's budget, held or failed calls, low-confidence judgements, repairs and rejections with their reason). Each item keeps the journal sequence number it comes from. A `strip` option removes the player's sentences and keeps their class.
2. **In the page** (`src/harvest.js` plus one delimited hook in `src/story.js` at session end and one in the legacy path where its journal is produced): build the record at the end of a session and on « Copy journal »; store it in the shared store when the page has one, else in the browser's storage (inside try/catch, capped, oldest dropped); a one-line notice the first time ("This session's journal is kept to improve the game. [Keep] [Don't keep]"), remembered; a « Harvest » entry in the debug panel listing the kept records with a copy button for all of them. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
3. **Out of the page**: `node tools/harvest.mjs <files or folder>` reads copied records or raw journals (both formats) and prints, and writes as Markdown under `harvest/`, the merged report: the most frequent requests that could not be served, grouped by what was missing; everything improvised, as ready-to-paste PDL source lines a creator could promote into a pack (with `?` or `~` strength and the provenance); the latency table by adapter and instrument. Add `story harvest` as an alias in `tools/story.mjs`. Document, in `docs/creator-kit.md`, how the coordinator pulls records out of the artifact's shared store (it has a tool for that; name the collection and the document shape precisely so it can).
4. Run your tool on the journal pasted in `docs/field/2026-11-20-free-session-phone.md` (reconstruct the entries from the note if the raw text is not enough, and say so) and on the golden journals, and commit the two reports as the first files of `harvest/`.

## Tests
Unit tests on the pure record builder with the golden journals and one small legacy fixture: the three lists, `strip`, the cap. No browser test beyond the single smoke run.
