## Deliver
1. **`src/model-port.js`**: the page's model port, a chain of adapters resolved per call (ADR 0008): the home node when one is configured and healthy (implement the client side only: address and token from the browser's storage, `/health` with a 1.5 s limit on load and every 30 s, per-capability state; no server in this lot), else the host's model when the page runs where one is offered (the artifact), else the reflex adapter that exists. A small indicator and every `model_call` journal entry say which adapter answered, whether the call was a judgement or an invention, and its latency.
2. **Judgement (`decide`)** for D1 (what is asked), D2 (now or later, under a condition) and D3 (does she go along), in one call when possible, asked only when the reflex match is below its threshold. Typed answers with a probability; a pure `judge()` in `core/` applies the confidence rule of the architecture (act, act with the doubt showing, ask back) and the both-orders rule on absolutes.
3. **Invention (`generate`)** for the scene loop: Sam's reaction to what was said, written by the model as PDL in the compact token form (ADR 0009), with her principles in scope, the examples and counter-examples, the station, the facts she knows and the commitments in a stable prompt prefix, and only the delta after it. Lines she invents are spoken in the play language. The answer is parsed, validated and repaired by the core like any transaction; a rejected line is journaled with the principle that rejected it. The now loop never waits for it (P20): the reflex or the line bank reacts first, and the model's answer lands as the scene loop and adds or amends. A time limit and a clean fallback when the model is slow or absent.
4. **The two endings that are Sam's own decision** become reachable when a model answers.
5. **Measured**: first reaction and full reaction latencies per call in the journal and in the debug panel, and a summary line at the end of a session (calls by adapter and kind, median latencies, repairs, rejections). Write in `docs/milestones.md` what you could measure here (nothing live: there is no model in this container) and how Toine measures it in the artifact.
6. The port change the scene loop needs (streaming lines if you do it, or not) written as an additive change to `core/ports.js` and noted for the spec.
