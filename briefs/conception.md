## Why

His words: « le modèle aurait dû écrire une histoire ou solliciter le créateur au moment de la conception pour avoir ses inputs ». ## Deliver
1. **The sketch** (`core/sketch.js`, pure): the prompt assembly that asks the large tier for a story sketch from the creator's sentence and the built place (its things and their names), and the reader of its answer. The answer is a small pack in the authoring source, as text: one carrier as a character sheet (who she or he is from the prompt, never "Sam" by default), the other people as light characters (three lines each) with where they are or why they are absent, a facts file (what is true, what the carrier knows and does not know), three open questions, one or two patterns chosen from the library with their roles filled, a handful of bank lines in the play language (greeting, refusal, asking back, doubt, hold), and what the engine cannot show yet as an explicit list (people, for instance). Then up to three **questions for the creator**, each with two or three suggested answers. The reader parses and compiles the sketch with `core/source.js`; a sketch that does not compile is repaired once by a second call with the errors, then falls back to a minimal sketch built in code from the place (one carrier, the place's things, two generic open questions), so play always starts.
2. **The creator's answers** become statements authored by the creator, in their own layer, on top of the model's layer: provenance is kept, and `story explain` shows who wrote what. "Surprise me" lets the model's own guess stand.
3. **In the page**: after a world is described and its place is built, a conception step in the project's art direction: the sketch summarised in a few lines (who is here, what is at stake, what will be played as absence), the questions with their chips and a "surprise me", then « Play ». It must be quick: the first question appears while the rest is still being written, and the creator can press « Play » at any time.
4. **The free world then runs on the core**: story mode with the generated pack (a single station built from the place when there are no stations; the place's things as things in reach; the reflex adapter for the immediate reaction; the model port for judgement and invention with the sketch in the stage prompt). The legacy free mode stays reachable as it is.
5. **Out of the page**: `node tools/story.mjs sketch "<sentence>" --place <place file> [--answers file]` writes the same sketch as a pack folder a creator can then edit, check and play with the existing commands (with no model it writes the code fallback and says so).
6. `docs/creation-pipeline.md` updated: which stages are now real, partly real, still staged.

## Tests
Unit tests with canned model answers: a good sketch compiles into a playable scene with two layers and provenance; a broken sketch is repaired by the second answer; a hopeless one falls back; the creator's answers override the model's guesses; the camp-fire prompt of the field note produces a carrier who is a father, a wife who is absent and two children as characters the engine cannot show. One short browser run of the conception step with a stand-in sampler.

## Order of work
The pure sketch and its tool first (commit), then the page step, then running the free world on the core. If the last part does not fit, deliver the first two with the sketch fed to the legacy free mode's prompt as its story, and say so.
