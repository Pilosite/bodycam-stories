## Question
The POC has four tiers that all speak PDL: reflex (code, < 100 ms), translation (0.2 to 0.8 s: player sentence → structured intent; intent → character line; admissibility), staging (2 to 8 s: the full reaction), background (continuous: story, branches, frontier of likely next intents). Toine, the project owner, keeps mentioning a family of models he half-remembers as "JEV": "LLMs that translate things instantly", and now says: "check again models like JEV or others; I think it is interesting for structuring events into structured data, in an 'AI' way, versus LLMs, even light ones". He is not sure of the name. The most likely referent is **JEPA** (joint-embedding predictive architectures; in particular recent vision-language variants that predict in embedding space instead of generating tokens and are pitched as real-time), but verify rather than assume, and cover the neighbouring options.
