The story (see docs/plan-poc.md §2, which Toine has not fully decided; make these choices and state them): a sober, warm, slightly mysterious short story, no horror, no farce. The Grands Avaux forest at Champcueil (Essonne), a November morning from sunrise to full light. Sam, 30, tree surgeon, dry and funny, not easily scared, knows trees but not this forest. Louis, her brother, forest ranger, did not come back from his round three weeks ago; his things were returned to her, including his bodycam. The player is the voice on the other end of the camera feed: Louis did his rounds with someone watching and talking; Sam turns the camera back on and someone is still there. The camera battery is the session clock. Three beats: the path (learning to talk to her; Louis's paint marks on trunks, which she reads better than we do), the platière (what he left; a choice of direction that the player proposes and Sam argues), the hide (what he was watching). What Louis was watching — choose the discreet version: alone and without telling anyone, he was following something fragile the forest shelters (decide precisely what, make it true to this forest and season; research with WebSearch if useful, text only), and his round stops because he chose to stay and watch; the story is grief turning into taking over the watch. Three families of endings, not three written scenes: she understands and takes over the round; she understands and turns the camera off; the battery dies first.
